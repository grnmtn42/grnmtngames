Angry Serfs

Storm the same escalating fortification across five levels -- pick your army each time, break the gate, take the keep.

How to Play

Objective

You command the attacking army. Storm the same fortress across 5 escalating levels -- break through the outer gate, then the keep's own gate, and defeat the defenders (and the King himself, waiting in his tower on level 5) to win. Survivors carry over into the next level at full health; buy new units and siege equipment with your budget before each battle.

Controls (desktop)

  • Left-click a unit to select it; drag across open ground to box-select a group; Shift+click to add or remove a unit from the selection.
  • Right-click a location to move there; right-click an enemy unit or structure to attack it.
  • Number keys 1-9 recall a control group; Ctrl+1-9 assigns the current selection to that group.
  • Move the mouse to the edge of the screen, or use the arrow keys, to pan the camera.

Controls (touch)

  • Tap a unit to select it; tap and hold, then drag, to box-select a group.
  • Tap open ground or an enemy with units selected to move or attack.
  • Drag the joystick (bottom-left) to pan the camera.
  • Use the unit buttons (bottom-right) to quickly select all units of one type.

Rules

  • Spend your budget on units (and, from level 2 on, siege equipment like the battering ram and catapult) in the shop before each battle.
  • Permadeath -- a unit that dies is gone for good. Survivors carry into the next level on top of that level's own budget, so keeping units alive pays off.
  • Siege equipment doesn't carry over between levels -- it's a one-battle rental, bought fresh each time.
  • Pick a difficulty before you start: harder difficulties shrink your budget bonus but multiply your final score.
  • Beat the whole game on any difficulty to submit a score to the leaderboard, and to unlock jumping straight to any level -- including at Impossible -- from here on.
